# How AAP Helps Customers

AAP (the **Azure Accelerate Program**) helps organizations adopt Azure faster, with less risk, and with clearer business outcomes.

## Why it matters to customers

- **Lower upfront cost:** AAP can provide funding support and incentives that reduce early project costs.
- **Faster time to value:** Customers get guided planning and execution support, so projects move from idea to delivery sooner.
- **Better decisions:** Customers receive practical guidance on priorities, so teams focus on high-impact work first.
- **Reduced risk:** Structured support helps avoid common migration and adoption mistakes.
- **Stronger internal capability:** Enablement sessions help teams build confidence and operate Azure more effectively.

## Summary of main benefits

| Main benefit | Customer value | Customer impact |
|---|---|---|
| Funding and incentives | Reduces initial financial pressure | Makes it easier to start or expand Azure projects |
| Expert guidance | Improves planning and execution quality | Fewer delays and clearer delivery plans |
| Accelerated adoption support | Speeds up implementation | Business outcomes are realized sooner |
| Risk reduction | Helps avoid common pitfalls | Lower chance of costly rework or downtime |
| Team enablement | Builds internal skills and confidence | More self-sufficient teams over time |

## Brief example scenario

A mid-sized retailer wants to modernize its sales and reporting systems on Azure, but is concerned about cost, speed, and execution risk. Through AAP, the company gets planning support, targeted incentives, and hands-on guidance. The project launches faster, early costs are reduced, and the team gains the skills to manage and grow the new platform.
